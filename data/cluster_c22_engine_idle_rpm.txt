# Cluster c_22: engine idle/rpm issue

Cluster c_22 is used for maintenance records related to engine idle/rpm issue.
The cluster should be assigned only when the problem description and corrective action provide enough evidence.

## Inclusion Signals

- rough idle
- low rpm
- high rpm
- idle override
- engine dies at idle
- idle adjustment
- fuel servo adjustment
- run-up normal
- throttle idle stop
- rpm instability

## Corrective Actions

- adjusted idle setting
- removed and replaced fuel servo
- performed engine run-up
- verified rpm within limits
- adjusted throttle linkage
- cleaned idle circuit

## Exclusion Rules

Exclude records that mention only a generic inspection with no corrective action.
Exclude records where the key component token is missing.
Exclude unrelated records that contain a similar word but describe a different component.
