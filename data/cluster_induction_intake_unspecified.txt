## Inclusion guidance

A record belongs to cluster induction_intake_unspecified when the maintenance problem and corrective action are consistent with `induction_intake unspecified`.
Strong evidence usually comes from both the PROBLEM field and the ACTION field.
A record should not be treated as a strong example if the problem text only contains a vague inspection note and the action does not describe a related corrective step.
